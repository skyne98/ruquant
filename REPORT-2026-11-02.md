# Overnight 2026-11-01/02: W4A4 Qwen3.8-27B on MI50

Pure W4A4 throughout: int4 weights × int4 activations, `v_dot8_i32_i4` on gfx906. No W4A8, no digit split.
The type map is unsloth's Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_0: its 4-bit layers become ours, the rest keeps unsloth's choice.
Only `ssm_out` and the lm_head moved up to Q8_0, for speed (see below).

## Bottom line

| | one MI50, box 1 | quality vs UD-Q8_K_XL (16 chunks) |
|---|---|---|
| unsloth Q4_0 + DFlash2 | 33.7 t/s mean, 42.8 best prompt | KLD 0.026, top-1 93.5% |
| **W4A4 (OSTQuant) + DFlash2** | **51.3 t/s mean, 66.6 best prompt (+52%)** | **KLD 0.0692, top-1 89.2%, PPL +5.3%** |
| W4A4 (Hadamard) + DFlash2 | 52.9 t/s mean, 65.2 best prompt (+57%) | KLD 0.0846, top-1 87.9%, PPL +4.6% |

- **Speed:** W4A4 verifies 6-8 tokens about 2× faster than Q4_0. A T=8 forward takes 47.1 ms against 98.9 ms.
  With speculative decoding that is +52-57% decode speed on one MI50, and prefill is +27% (364 vs 287 t/s at 512).
- **140 t/s on one MI50 is not reachable with this model and drafter.** Reading 16 GB of weights per verify
  step costs ≥19 ms even at full HBM bandwidth. Add the drafter, plus mx's recurrent-state bookkeeping
  (~10 ms/step), against ~4.4 accepted tokens per step at best.
- **TP:** no gain on box 1 (ROCm 5.7, PCIe gen3 through the CPU). TP=2 matches one GPU; TP=4 crashes in
  mx's custom all-reduce, whose stream capture ROCm 5.7 rejects.
- **Quality is still short of Q4_0:** KLD 0.069 vs 0.026, top-1 89.2% vs 93.5%. PPL is +5% vs Q4_0's +2.2%. That is in line with
  published W4A4 results, but not at the Q4_0 bar.

| final Hadamard layout, GPTQ against quantized-activation Hessians (`hessian --act-clip`) | 0.0831 | 88.1% | 1.055 |
| final OSTQuant layout at training step 64 instead of 45 | 0.0730 | 88.9% | 1.054 |

- **OSTQuant port** (`accuracy/ostquant_qwen.py`, the repo's recipe) works once GPTQ follows, as in the paper:
  KLD 0.0739 vs 0.0840 for plain Hadamard + GPTQ, 12% lower.
  - With RTN weights it looked worse (0.194 vs 0.150). The smoothing shifts difficulty onto the weights,
    which GPTQ absorbs, so my RTN mid-check was the wrong proxy and I stopped training at step 45 because of it.
  - geoopt RiemannianAdam normalizes the rotation's step by the whole-matrix gradient norm, so R barely moves.
    The learned part is mostly the smoothing scales.
  - More training doesn't help with this recipe. Resumed to step 64, the same final layout scores KLD 0.0730 /
    88.9%, against 0.0692 / 89.2% at step 45. The step-45 model stays the best.
- **Activation clip matters:** 0.8 on the norm outputs and 0.7 on the Hadamard-rotated inputs (attn_output,
  ffn_down). The clips now live in the model, in every W4A4 block header (`ruquant setclip`). The
  `RQ_ACT_CLIP(_H)` environment variables still override them.

## Speed (box 1, one MI50 32 GB, ROCm 5.7, `GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_GRAPHS=1`)

Forward pass, ms per batch of T tokens (llama-bench):

| | T=1 | T=6 | T=8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| unsloth Q4_0 | 37.9 | 84.5 | 98.9 |
| W4A4 (q4map) | 35.5 | 44.2 | 50.5 |
| W4A4 final (Q8_0 ssm_out + head) | 36.8 | 43.8 | 47.1 |

Prefill (pp512): Q4_0 287 t/s, W4A4 364 t/s. Plain decode without a drafter (tg128): Q4_0 27.0, W4A4 27.7 t/s.
At one token per pass both are bandwidth-bound; W4A4's gain is in multi-token passes.

The W4A4 matmuls are bit-exact with the CPU reference on the MI50 (test-backend-ops, 58 shapes).
How they got fast:
- `v_dot8` skinny kernel with an in-place weight repack on first use, plus a fused epilogue and split-K fixup;
- tiled Paley stage for the online Hadamard;
- launch shape from a sweep: 8 waves, 4-way split-K.

Spec decode (llama-server, chat prompts, greedy, 256 tokens, 4 prompts):
- DFlash2 (incoai) beats DSpark here: about 45-69% acceptance, mean accepted length up to 4.4.
- Draft length 5 is best for W4A4. Each extra draft token costs ~4 ms per step.
- The drafter is converted for the rotated target by `ruquant drafter`:
  - R1 is folded into its `fc` input chunks;
  - it gets its own unrotated token embedding;
  - its own lm_head is W4A4H (online Hadamard), and acceptance is unchanged;
  - `--linears w4a4h` also makes its layers W4A4H: small gain.
- Q8_0 `ssm_out` and Q8_0 lm_head instead of Q5_K / q6_K cut a step from 69 to 65 ms. mx's repacked Q5_K
  kernel at 6 columns took 150 µs per call. Both types are more accurate than the ones they replace.
- Backend (GPU) sampling: +1 t/s.
- Sustained 1024-token generations: 55.2 t/s mean, 70.9 t/s on the math prompt; the step time stays at 64.5 ms.

Where a step's ~65 ms goes:
- target W4A4 GEMMs ~23 ms;
- mx recurrent-state `set_rows` and copies ~10 ms;
- drafter ~13 ms;
- activation quantizers ~4 ms;
- Paley ~2.7 ms;
- DeltaNet, norms, attention and the rest ~12 ms.

## Reproduce

- Build on an MI50 box: `rig/build_mx.sh`, which applies `patches/mx-llama.cpp-ruquant.patch` to mx-llama.cpp
  c4d12e2, ROCm 5.7 fixes included.
- OSTQuant: `.venv/bin/python accuracy/ostquant_qwen.py --out models/ost` (2.3 min/step on the 3090), then
  `accuracy/ost_pipeline.sh`, or the steps below with `--ost models/ost`.
- Model: `ruquant convert UD-Q8_K_XL.gguf OUT [--ost DIR] --types-from Q4_0.gguf --set ssm_out=q8_0
  --set output.weight=q8_0 --hessians DIR`, with the Hessians from `ruquant hessian` on the same model rounded
  (no `--hessians`). Then `ruquant setclip OUT 0.8 0.7`.
- Drafter, with the target's R1 (`--ost DIR` or `--seed 1`):
  `ruquant drafter DFlash2-Q8_0.gguf Q4_0.gguf OUT --head w4a4h --linears w4a4h`, then `ruquant setclip OUT 0.8 0.8`.
- Bench: `rig/spec_bench.sh MODEL DRAFTER 5` with `SPEC=draft-dflash EXTRA="-bs --samplers temperature"`.
- Score on a box: `rig/score_box.sh`.
